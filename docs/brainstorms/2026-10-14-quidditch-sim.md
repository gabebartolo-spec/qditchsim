# Brainstorm: Quidditch sim concept (2026-10-14, big)

## The idea (director's words)
"Inspired by the simplistic beauty of Golden Lap and my development of Aussie Rules
Dynasties, I started wondering what a quidditch sports sim could look like."

The main thing borrowed from Golden Lap is the look: "a stylised, beautiful, simple
quidditch match."

## The moment in play
The player is a team manager watching a stylised isometric match. Players fly over the
pitch with ground shadows showing their height. While the match runs, the manager adjusts
three tactical dials live, at any time:
- when the Seeker goes for the Snitch
- who the Beaters target
- how far the Chasers push forward versus protecting the Keeper

Over a career, the manager starts with a Hogwarts house team and, by winning there,
advances to the pro league.

## Decided
- Player role is team manager, covering both squad/seasons and tactics: director's choice
  ("both"), deliberately broader than ARD's coach role.
- Match view is isometric, with dynamic camera moments: director's picture of the match.
- Dynamic camera moments trigger on goals, Snitch sightings and Bludger hits: director
  accepted these examples ("yeah exactly like you said").
- Three tactical dials, layered (Snitch timing, Beater targets, attack vs defend): no
  single dial is the heart of the match.
- Dials can be changed anytime, live, during the match.
- Career starts at Hogwarts; winning there advances you to the pro league.
- Reputation, players you developed and your manager skills carry over to the pro league.
- Core test: does the feature make the climb from house team to pro dynasty more
  meaningful? The director chose "my dynasty" as what players should remember most.
- Free fan project: keep Hogwarts and Quidditch names, never sell it.
- Height in the isometric view is shown with ground shadows.
- First playable build is one match only, to prove the match is fun before any
  management layer.
- Dynamic camera moments are left out of the first build.

## Rejected and why
- Changing dials only at key moments, or before the match only: director chose live.
- Original names, or deciding the rights question later: director chose a free fan project.
- Hogwarts-only or thin-both-stages first build: director chose one match only.
- Height stalks, or ignoring height: director chose ground shadows.
- Fresh start in the pro league: director wants reputation, players and skills to carry over.
- Cutting player stats/traits or fouls from the first build: director kept them in.

## Open questions for the director
- The look beyond "stylised, beautiful, simple": palette, era feel, how players and
  the pitch are drawn. Not described yet, and not to be guessed.
- Scope: the full design is two career stages, a management layer, three live dials and
  an animated match. That is a lot against Golden Lap's simplicity.
- Rights: a free fan project still carries takedown risk. Confirm you accept that.
- What player stats and traits exist, and how they affect the three dials.
- Which fouls and rules the first match includes.
- How ARD's in-match work (still in progress) informs this match design.
- No vision, pillars or hard-rules document exists yet for this project.
